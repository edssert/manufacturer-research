# 24C-E

Status: pending. The product is a passive column extender and is explicitly not intended to operate as a stand-alone system.

Sources reviewed:

- `raw-data/official-docs/db/speakers/references/installation-integrated-sound-solutions-en.pdf`, pp. 31-32, SHA-256 `043836e9d4191fab62366b5986325f53181b39e7533b8f6e893b322a9c121d54`.
- d&b official 24C/24C-E Manual 1.4 en: `https://www.dbaudio.com/assets/products/downloads/manuals-documentation/xc-series/dbaudio-manual-24c-24c-e-1.4-en.pdf`, sections 2.1.2, 2.3 and 2.4, reviewed 2026-08-18.

Verified extender-only facts: 6 x 4 in neodymium drivers; 125 W RMS / 600 W peak; fixed Mate-N-Lok connection through 24C; 701 x 124.5 x 124 mm; 7 kg. The manual says the extender is electrically connected through the 24C without an additional amplifier channel.

Missing independent card fields: stand-alone maximum SPL, stand-alone frequency response, stand-alone nominal horizontal/vertical coverage, and stand-alone amplifier configuration. The published 128 dB, 90 x 20 deg, 110 Hz-17 kHz and one-cabinet-per-channel values describe the combined `24C + 24C-E` system and must not be assigned to the extender alone.
