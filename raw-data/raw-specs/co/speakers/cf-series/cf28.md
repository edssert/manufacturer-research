## Card facts

- Format / topology: point source / three-way, single-channel passive
- Drivers: LF 2 × 8 in cone; MF 1 × 3 in compression; HF 1 × 2 in compression
- Max output: 142 dB SPL at 1 m
- Coverage: 120° H × 50° V, rotatable
- Frequency: 46 Hz–20 kHz (-10 dB); 52 Hz–19 kHz (±3 dB)
- Weight: 20.9 kg
- Amplification / network: Cohesion-approved amplifier with custom Lake DSP; network control belongs to the external processing system
- Connection: 2 × NL4; 1 × 4-pin terminal block
- Dimensions H × W × D: 247.7 × 605.2 × 349.3 mm
- Archived datasheet: `raw-data/official-docs/co/datasheets/cf28-datasheet.pdf`

The archived 2025 datasheet contains an older 137 dB statement. The current official product page publishes 142 dB and is used by the card.
