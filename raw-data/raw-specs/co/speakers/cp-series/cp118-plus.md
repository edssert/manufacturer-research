## Card facts

- Format / topology: self-powered subwoofer
- Drivers: LF 1 × 18 in cone
- Max output: 140 dB SPL at 1 m
- Single-enclosure coverage: omnidirectional. Cardioid operation requires a multi-enclosure array and DSP configuration; it is not intrinsic to one cabinet.
- Frequency: 24 Hz–90 Hz (-10 dB); 29 Hz–80 Hz (±3 dB)
- Weight: 59.1 kg
- Amplification / network: current product page states onboard 5,000 W; official input list publishes analog XLR only
- Connection: 2 × XLR3, one male and one female; powerCON TRUE1 in/link
- Dimensions H × W × D: 723.9 × 504.6 × 569.7 mm
- Archived datasheet: `raw-data/official-docs/co/datasheets/cp118-plus-datasheet.pdf`

The archived datasheet states 8,500 W while the current product page states 5,000 W. The current page value is used by the card and the discrepancy remains explicit.
