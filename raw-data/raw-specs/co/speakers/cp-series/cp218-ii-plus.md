## Card facts

- Format / topology: self-powered subwoofer
- Drivers: LF 2 × 18 in cone
- Max output: 146 dB SPL at 1 m
- Single-enclosure coverage: omnidirectional. Cardioid operation requires a multi-enclosure array and DSP configuration; it is not intrinsic to one cabinet.
- Frequency standard mode: 28 Hz–72 Hz (-10 dB); 34 Hz–60 Hz (±3 dB)
- Frequency ULF mode: 26 Hz–72 Hz (-10 dB); 28 Hz–60 Hz (±3 dB)
- Weight: 139 kg
- Amplification / network: onboard 9,000 W; official input list publishes analog XLR only
- Connection: 2 × XLR3, one male and one female; powerCON TRUE1 in/link
- Dimensions H × W × D: 563.2 × 1216.2 × 810.4 mm
