# Ti-SUB

Source: `raw-data/official-docs/db/speakers/references/installation-integrated-sound-solutions-en.pdf`, pp. 38-39, SHA-256 `043836e9d4191fab62366b5986325f53181b39e7533b8f6e893b322a9c121d54`.

Installation-specific passive omnidirectional subwoofer; 1 x 15 in; 130 dB maximum SPL with 30D/D80/40D; 47 Hz-140 Hz (-5 dB); 300 W RMS; 2 cabinets per amplifier channel; 431 x 470 x 400 mm; 17 kg. The brochure states it may be ground stacked or flown above a Ti10L array.
