# Bi10N-SUB

Source: `raw-data/official-docs/db/speakers/u-series/system-brochure-en.pdf`, D1418.EN.02 (04/2026), pp. 4, 6.

Installation-specific networked active equivalent of B10N-SUB; bass-reflex; 2 × 10 in; omnidirectional; 130 dB max SPL; 38 Hz–150 Hz (-5 dB), 34 Hz–200 Hz (-10 dB); 500 W RMS / 1200 W peak (10 ms); 28.5 kg. Onboard amplification accepts Milan-AVB or analog audio and AC or PoE++ power.
