# Ashby-8C

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/ashby-series/ashby-8c-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way coaxial ceiling point source with IntelligentDC; active crossover; integral class-D amplifier
- Transducers: 1 × 8 in cone; 1 × 0.75 in dome tweeter mounted concentrically in a waveguide
- Operating range: 60 Hz–18 kHz
- Frequency response: 67 Hz–16 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 100° conical
- Weight: 6.26 kg
- Dimensions: 321 mm outside diameter × 225.3 mm depth
- Connectors: two Phoenix 5-pin male, input + hardwired loop output; balanced audio + 48 V DC
- Amplifier output: 440 W peak
- Self-powered: yes; onboard amplifier/DSP, with external Meyer Sound MPS IntelligentDC 48 V power supply required

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is **114 dB** with M-noise (19 dB crest factor), 106.5 dB with pink noise, and 109.5 dB with B-noise. It is measured at 4 m and referred to 1 m, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores 114 dB unchanged.
