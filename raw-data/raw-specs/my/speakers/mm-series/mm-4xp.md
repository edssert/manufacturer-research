# MM-4XP

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/mm-series/mm-4xp-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 1-way miniature point source with IntelligentDC; integral class-D amplifier and signal processing
- Transducer: 1 × 4 in long-excursion cone (4 Ω)
- Operating range: 120 Hz–18 kHz
- Frequency response: 135 Hz–17 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 80° horizontal × 80° vertical at 3–14 kHz (±10°); 120° × 120° below 2 kHz
- Weight: 1.9 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 103 × 103 × 145 mm
- Connector: SwitchCraft EN3 5-pin male; balanced audio + 48 V DC
- Amplifier output: 440 W peak
- Self-powered: yes; onboard amplifier/DSP, with external Meyer Sound IntelligentDC 48 V power supply required

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is 111.5 dB with M-noise (>16.5 dB crest factor), 109 dB with pink noise, and 111 dB with B-noise. It is measured free-field at 4 m, referred to 1 m, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores **111 dB**, rounding 111.5 down to avoid overstatement.
