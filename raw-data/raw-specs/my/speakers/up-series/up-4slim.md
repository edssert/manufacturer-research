# UP-4slim

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/up-series/up-4slim-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way point source with IntelligentDC; active crossover; integral 3-channel class-D amplifier
- Transducers: 2 × 4 in LF cone (4 Ω); 1 × 1 in metal-dome tweeter (8 Ω)
- Operating range: 65 Hz–18 kHz
- Frequency response: 70 Hz–18 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 100° horizontal × 100° vertical
- Weight: 6.35 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 109 × 404 × 144 mm
- Connectors: Phoenix 5-pin male; balanced audio + 48 V DC
- Amplifier output: 500 W peak
- Weather option: IPx5 with correctly installed rain hood
- Self-powered: yes; onboard amplifier/DSP, with external Meyer Sound IntelligentDC 48 V power supply required

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is 116.5 dB with M-noise (>16.5 dB crest factor), 109.5 dB with pink noise, and 112.5 dB with B-noise. It is measured free-field at 4 m, referred to 1 m, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores **116 dB**, rounding 116.5 down to avoid overstatement.
