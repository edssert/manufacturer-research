# UPM-1XP

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/up-series/upm-1xp-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way point source with IntelligentDC; active crossover; integral 3-channel class-D amplifier
- Transducers: 2 × 5 in LF cone (8 Ω); 1 × 1 in metal-dome tweeter (8 Ω)
- Operating range: 75 Hz–20 kHz
- Frequency response: 80 Hz–16 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 100° horizontal × 100° vertical
- Weight: 7.7 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 174 × 457 × 196 mm
- Connectors: Phoenix 5-pin male, SwitchCraft EN3 5-pin male, or ECO-M 7-pin male; balanced audio + 48 V DC
- Amplifier output: 650 W peak
- Self-powered: yes; onboard amplifier/DSP, with external Meyer Sound IntelligentDC 48 V power supply required

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is 118.5 dB with M-noise (>14 dB crest factor), 113 dB with pink noise, and 115.5 dB with B-noise. It is measured free-field at 4 m, referred to 1 m, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores **118 dB**, rounding 118.5 down to avoid overstatement.
