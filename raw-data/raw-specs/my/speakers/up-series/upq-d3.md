# UPQ-D3

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/up-series/upq-d3-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way point source; active crossover; integral 2-channel class-D amplifier
- Transducers: 1 × 15 in neodymium LF cone (2 Ω); 1 × 4 in diaphragm compression driver (8 Ω)
- Operating range: 55 Hz–18 kHz
- Frequency response: 57 Hz–18 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 80° horizontal × 80° vertical at the -6 dB points (100° × 100° at -10 dB)
- Weight: 43 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 483 × 715 × 462 mm
- Connectors: XLR 3-pin female input / male loop output; optional 5-pin audio+RMS; powerCON 20 AC input / loop output
- Amplifier output: 2250 W peak
- Self-powered: yes

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is **135 dB** with M-noise (>17 dB crest factor), 132 dB with pink noise, and 134 dB with B-noise. It is measured free-field at 4 m, referred to 1 m, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores 135 dB unchanged.
