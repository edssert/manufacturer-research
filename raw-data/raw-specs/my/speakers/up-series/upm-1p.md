# UPM-1P

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/up-series/upm-1p-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way point source; active crossover; integral 2-channel class-AB/bridged amplifier
- Transducers: 2 × 5 in LF cone (8 Ω); 1 × 1 in metal-dome tweeter (8 Ω)
- Operating range: 75 Hz–20 kHz
- Frequency response: 80 Hz–16 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 100° horizontal × 100° vertical
- Weight: 9.98 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 176 × 457 × 193 mm
- Connectors: XLR 3-pin female input / male loop output; optional 5-pin audio+RMS; powerCON AC input / loop output
- Amplifier output: 650 W peak
- Self-powered: yes

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is 118.5 dB with M-noise (>14 dB crest factor), 113 dB with pink noise, and 115.5 dB with B-noise. The manufacturer measures Linear Peak SPL free-field at 4 m, referred to 1 m, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores **118 dB**, rounding 118.5 down to avoid overstatement.
