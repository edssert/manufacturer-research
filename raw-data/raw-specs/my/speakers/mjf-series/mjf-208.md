# MJF-208

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/mjf-series/mjf-208-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way stage monitor; active crossover; integral 3-channel class-D amplifier
- Transducers: 2 × 8 in high-power LF cone (4 Ω); 1 × 3 in diaphragm compression driver (16 Ω)
- Operating range: 55 Hz–18 kHz
- Frequency response: 60 Hz–18 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 70° horizontal × 50° vertical
- Weight: 21.3 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 474 × 322 × 472 mm
- Connectors: XLR 3-pin female input / male loop output; optional XLR 5-pin audio+RMS; powerCON 20 AC input / loop output
- Amplifier output: 1770 W peak
- Self-powered: yes

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is **129 dB** with M-noise (>16 dB crest factor), 128 dB with pink noise, and 129 dB with B-noise. The Linear Peak table footnote specifies free-field at 4 m referred to 1 m, M-noise at the onset of limiting, while the architectural specification also describes 1 m on-axis with typical boundary loading. Both official locators are retained because the measurement wording differs. The card stores 129 dB unchanged.
