# MJF-210

Source: locked Meyer Sound datasheet `raw-data/official-docs/my/speakers/mjf-series/mjf-210-datasheet.html` (DOM specification table, product description, footnotes, and architectural specification).

- Type / network: self-powered 2-way stage monitor; active crossover; integral 3-channel class-D amplifier
- Transducers: 2 × 10 in high-power LF cone (4 Ω); 1 × 4 in diaphragm compression driver (8 Ω)
- Operating range: 55 Hz–18 kHz
- Frequency response: 60 Hz–16 kHz ±4 dB
- Coverage: 50° horizontal × 70° vertical
- Weight: 30.4 kg
- Dimensions (W × H × D): 589 × 353 × 531 mm
- Connectors: XLR 3-pin female input / male loop output; optional XLR 5-pin audio+RMS; powerCON 20 AC input / loop output
- Amplifier output: 1950 W peak
- Self-powered: yes

## SPL selection

No Manufacturer Maximum SPL or AES75 dBZpk value is published. Linear Peak SPL is **134 dB** with M-noise (>17 dB crest factor), 132.5 dB with pink noise, and 132.5 dB with B-noise. It is measured 1 m on-axis with typical boundary loading, with M-noise at the onset of limiting; compression after two hours at 50 °C is under 2 dB. The card stores 134 dB unchanged.
